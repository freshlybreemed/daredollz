DARE DOLLZ

Dare Dollz began as a line of collectible dolls inspired by the grit, glamour, and contradictions of street culture. Today, it's a growing universe — spanning comics, collectibles, and apparel.

Set in the hyper-advanced city of Carnado, Dare Dollz follows Demi, Paris, Chanel, and Bleu — stylish social media icons living double lives as elite covert agents. Behind the filters and fame, they navigate loyalty, power, and identity while tracking one of the city's most dangerous figures: the elusive criminal mastermind and Carnado's favorite rapper, 8 Figga.
